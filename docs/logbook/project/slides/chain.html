What we are building

  • A · Geant4: particle through the full wedge → ionization clusters in the gas (position, time, primaries, where it converted)
  • B · Digitizer: drift, diffusion, attachment, mesh transparency, avalanche gain, pad lookup → charge per pad
  • C · VMM3a: shaper (Iakovidis et al.), threshold → amplitude and time; gain 3.0 / 4.5 mV/fC, 100 / 200 ns
  • Garfield++ feeds B: Magboltz gas tables, avalanche gain vs mesh voltage (to calibrate on SPS HV scans), mesh transparency, induced pad signal → into C
  • COMSOL feeds Garfield: the electric field around the woven mesh (unit cell)
Geant4 is stage A. Garfield supplies the gas physics and signal used in B and passed to C. COMSOL provides the field map near the mesh wires, which Garfield needs for transparency and local gain.
