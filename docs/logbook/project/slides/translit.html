Transparency vs drift field (corrected cell)

  • Every gas is flat up to E_drift/E_amp ≈ 0.016 (≈ 450 V/cm here) and then falls; hot mixtures (Ar or Ne with 5 % isobutane) fall first
  • P2 at SPS (ratio 0.022) is just past the plateau for the 95/5 mixes (−6 %), on it for 10–15 % isobutane and the SPS gases
  • Ar/iso 95/5 at 450 V/cm (drift −630 V on a 4 mm gap): 99.3 % instead of 94.3 %, at the cost of a slower drift
2 mm cell, mesh bottom on the 150 µm pillars, mesh −450 V (E_amp 28.5 kV/cm), one COMSOL solve per drift field, 3000 electrons each, interface-crossing check and null-collision steps.
