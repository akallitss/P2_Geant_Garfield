Heed vs Geant4, like-for-like: the same bare gas slab

  • Same box in both codes: 4 mm Ar/iso, nothing around it (new mm_sim -m slab)
  • Geant4 PAI = Heed in mean and median only; shape differs: Heed more peaked, Geant4 wider
  • Below 10 e⁻: Geant4 4.1 % vs Heed 1.8 % (×2.3, PAI and option4 alike): this is the VMM-loss region, open
  • Urban (option4): mean +3 %, peak too high
  • PIXE (on in our physics list) adds +8 %, and more with the 100 µm step limit: +18 % in total
  • Muons: no PIXE in Geant4, so µ agreed all along
Answer to Dylan's MX17 handoff (15 Oct). Geant4 11.2.1 option4, 100 MeV e- at 0 deg, 100k events per point; Heed: scripts/garfield/heed_mip.cc, same 100k, seed 1, identical to Dylan's numbers. PIXE + Penelope e- ionisation double-counts atomic de-excitation (Geant4 warns at start-up); it also makes the result depend on the step length. With PIXE off our slab reproduces Dylan's slab.cc: opt4 41.16/32 vs 41.27/32, PAI 39.63/29 vs 39.78/29. Also checked: Ar/CF4/iso, Ne/iso (median = Heed), 30 mm, 150 GeV mu. AddPAIModel works on 11.2.1 (no-op only in 11.3). Scripts: scripts/analysis/heed_vs_g4_slab.py, heed_vs_g4_slab_plot.py; results docs/results/heed/pai.
