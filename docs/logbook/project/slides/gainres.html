Gain: simulation vs data

  • Absolute gain matches with a normal Penning rate: r ≈ 0.36 at 150 µm (0.31 at 120 µm, 0.39 at 165 µm) for Ar/CO2/iso; r ≈ 0.35 for Ar/iso from Fe55 — the gain alone cannot tell the gap
  • Slope: simulated e-folding 31–35 V for every gap, measured 22–27 V (SPS P2_OUT) and 28 V (Fe55): the data are steeper, not explained by the gap
  • Wire Ø 16/20 µm and spacing 66/73 µm change the gain by < 10 %
Simulation: mesh_gain.cc, Townsend alpha (Magboltz, Penning-adjusted) integrated along RKF drift lines through the COMSOL cell, mean gain over a 20x20 start grid; geometry DAG 12548862; results docs/results/gain/geom_gain_lines.txt, r fits penning_r_from_gain.csv. Data: DREAM 200 fC range (registers 6/7 = 0xAAAA), 18 ADC/fC (Mandjavidze transfer-function slides); SPS P2_OUT beam_nominal_meshscan_1 Landau MPV / (17-27 primaries x T 0.975); det2 Fe55 photopeak / 227; both divided by the ion-charge fraction seen at the 180 ns peak (0.46-0.76, central 0.6). Caveats: constant r assumed (real r can vary with field), no avalanche fluctuations or space charge; the steeper measured slope could point to a field-dependent r, the SPS MPV near threshold, or charging. Script: scripts/analysis/gain_vs_data.py.
