Answers

efficiency, e/γ cut, rates (× P2Sim)

Orange = Matthieu's environment simulation; grey = inputs we generate; blue = our detector chain. P2Sim tells us which photons arrive (energy, angle, rate); our Geant4 tells us what each one does inside the detector; Garfield (with the COMSOL field and Magboltz tables) turns that into charge and signal; the VMM emulator turns it into hits. The rates come back in at the end: our per-photon response × P2Sim flux.
