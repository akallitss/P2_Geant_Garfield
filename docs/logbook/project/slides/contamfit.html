SPS drift scan: contamination fit

  • Model: free scale × O2 attachment over the drift × our transparency (dry / humid) × 180 ns shaping → fits the whole scan (P2_OUT χ² 2–6 / 6, P2_MID 12–15 / 6)
  • Once a low-field loss is allowed, the high-field “edge too early” largely disappears: normalising to the maximum was biased by the low-field loss
  • But it needs O2 ≈ 4200–4600 ppm-eq (P2_OUT) and 2000–2250 (P2_MID): implausibly high and different per chamber; water barely matters → not proven
Data: drift_mesh_scan_1, Ar/CO2/iso 93/5/2, mesh 450 V, DREAM Landau MPV (stage 20). Attachment: Magboltz tables for Ar/CO2/iso + O2 100/300/1000 ppm (eta linear in ppm), averaged over a uniform track in the 3.96 mm gap; transparency: Garfield, corrected cell, dry and with 500/2000 ppm H2O; shaping: CR-RC^4, 180 ns, box of width d/v_d. Caveat: Magboltz treats three-body O2 attachment with O2 as third body only; with CO2/isobutane the real rate per ppm can be larger, so the ppm-eq is an upper bound on the real O2. No O2/H2O monitor at SPS, ~4 l/h, plastic tubing. Cross-check next: the cosmic det1 scan (little low-field loss, still falls at high field).
