Validation · their mesh, their gas, their data

  • Same COMSOL cell rebuilt with their geometry (63.5 µm spacing, 128 µm gap, calendered 30 µm, E_amp 41.6 kV/cm vs their 41.4) and their gas
  • Our simulation follows the measurement: rms deviation 2.4 % with null-collision steps (max 3.8 %), 3.4 % with default steps (max 6.6 %); their own calculation agreed to 5 %
  • So the earlier 3–9 % offset came from comparing different geometries, not from the method
Nikolopoulos, Bhattacharya, Chernyatin, Veenhof, JINST 6 (2011) P06011 (arXiv:2005.01065), fig. 5, points read off the figure (±0.02); data and simulation normalised below 600 V/cm. 4000 electrons per point.
