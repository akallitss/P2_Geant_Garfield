Mesh transparency per gas (Garfield, SPS fields)

  • Field lines alone: 100 % in every gas. The losses come from diffusion pushing electrons off the field lines onto the wires
  • More isobutane → cooler electrons, less diffusion: Ar 95/5 → 90/10 gives +9 % signal; Ne 95/5 → 85/15 gives +7 %
  • SPS gases: 95–96 %
  • 2 mm and 4 mm cells agree within errors
5000 electrons per point, started uniformly 150 µm above the mesh and tracked collision by collision (AvalancheMicroscopic, pinned Garfield build 927e5c21 so electrons cannot step through the wires) until they pass below the wires or stop on one. Drift 632 V/cm, gap 31.5 kV/cm (mesh −450 V, drift −700 V), 1 atm, 20 °C. Error bars: binomial. This replaces the 100 % placeholder in stage B.
