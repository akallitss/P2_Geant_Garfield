Two Geant4 simulations, two questions

P2Sim · the whole experiment

"What arrives at the detector?"

  • Beam, target, chamber walls, shielding
  • Micromegas as simple 8 × 8 mm boxes, one gas
  • Output: photons per layer: energy, angle, rate (MHz)

Ours · one detailed wedge

"What does the detector see?"

  • Every layer from the design files: real mesh, pads, pillars, frame
  • One photon energy or 100 MeV electrons at a time, 4 gases, 2 gaps
  • Output per event: where it converts, energy deposited, which pads

Background rate in a layer = Σ over energies [ P2Sim flux(E) ] × [ our P(detected | E) ]

Signal: the same chain with 100 MeV electrons. The deposit spectra of both give the upper ADC cut.

Why not only P2Sim: it uses one gas and one gap, simplified materials (45 µm mesh pitch, 5× our copper), no pads or pillars, and gives no event-by-event deposits. Its job is the environment; ours is the detector response. Neither replaces the other.
